Privacy Policy

Publisher: Ramiz Kichibekov

NVMe Monitor processes external SSD readings on your Mac. It does not automatically send them to the publisher or third parties.

Drive information and preferences

The app reads device details such as the model, connection type, capacity and current macOS disk identifier, plus SMART temperatures, warnings, errors and usage counters. It does not open drive files, store serial numbers or retain a history of SMART readings. Preferences, the warning state and a boot-session identifier are stored locally to restore your choices, avoid duplicate warnings and distinguish drive connections.

Optional MetricKit diagnostics

macOS may provide performance reports and technical diagnostics such as crash or hang traces. The app does not add drive readings, identifiers or preferences. It keeps up to five reports from the last seven days locally. Use Settings → Support & Privacy to preview or delete them. Temporary attachment copies are cleared on subsequent launches or when you delete reports. Copies saved or sent by your mail app are managed separately.

Support email

Write Email opens an editable draft with an empty recipient field and the app and macOS versions. The optional diagnostics JSON is included only if you select it. Review or remove it before sending. The app does not send the email or upload reports. If your mail app cannot attach the file, you can attach the local copy yourself. The publisher uses what you choose to share to respond and investigate the issue.

Notifications and launch at login

If you allow notifications, macOS delivers them and manages their visibility. They may show the drive model and the relevant reading or warning. Launch at login is optional and macOS manages the registration.

Collection, retention and your choices

The app requires no account and uses no advertising, tracking or analytics. It does not sell your data. Settings and warning state stay local until updated or removed. Use macOS to revoke notification permission, clear delivered alerts or remove the login item. To remove saved settings and warning state, remove local app data when uninstalling.

Website and support services

Cloudflare hosts this site and processes connection information to serve and protect it. The site uses no advertising or analytics scripts. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards support email to the publisher’s Gmail inbox. The providers process messages and attachments to deliver and store them.

The site saves your appearance and temperature-unit preferences in your browser.

Contact us with privacy questions or to request deletion of information you shared by email. support@nvmemonitor.app
