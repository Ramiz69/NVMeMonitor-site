Which drives work?

NVMe Monitor needs macOS 27 or later and an external SSD that macOS can see as native NVMe. Compatible Thunderbolt and USB4 enclosures can expose the controller over PCIe. Some enclosures hide SMART data even when Finder can access your files.

My drive or its SMART data is missing

  1. Confirm the SSD appears as a physical drive in Disk Utility.
  2. Connect the enclosure directly with an appropriate Thunderbolt or USB4 cable, if supported.
  3. Choose Refresh in NVMe Monitor. If your manually selected drive has reconnected, select it again in Settings → Monitoring.

Notifications and temperature units

Enable notifications in Settings → Alerts and allow them in macOS. In Settings → Monitoring, choose Celsius, Fahrenheit, or the system setting.

Optional diagnostics

In Settings → Support & Privacy, you can preview or clear local MetricKit reports. Choose Attach MetricKit Diagnostics if you wish to share a report. Enter the support address in your email draft and review the message and attachment before sending.
