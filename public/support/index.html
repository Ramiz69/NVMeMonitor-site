Which drives work?

NVMe Monitor requires macOS 27 or later and an external NVMe SSD that macOS can see as native NVMe. Compatible Thunderbolt and USB4 enclosures can expose the controller over a PCIe connection. Some enclosures hide SMART data even when Finder can access your files.

My drive or its SMART data is missing

  • Confirm the SSD appears as a physical drive in Disk Utility.
  • Use a directly connected port and an appropriate Thunderbolt or USB4 cable if your enclosure supports them.
  • Open NVMe Monitor and choose Refresh. If you had manually selected a drive that reconnected, choose it again under Settings → Monitoring.

Notifications and temperature units

Enable notifications in Settings → Alerts and allow them in macOS. Under Settings → Monitoring, choose Celsius, Fahrenheit or your Mac’s regional setting.

Optional diagnostics

In Settings → Support & Privacy, you can preview or clear local MetricKit reports. Choose Attach MetricKit Diagnostics before writing an email if you want to include a report. Enter the support address above in your mail draft, and review the message and attachment before sending.
